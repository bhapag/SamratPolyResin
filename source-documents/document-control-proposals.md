# Document-control proposals (India site)

Prepared 2026-09-14. These are proposed corrections to issued documents. None has been applied to a
published PDF: each needs the issuer (Samrat technical/document control) to approve and reissue.
The website states the current documents truthfully in the meantime, so no website work waits on these.

## 2. ISO Gelcoat thixotropic index (SPR-TDS-IGC Rev. 01, September 2026)

- Printed: Viscosity low shear 40,000 cPs; high shear 1,500 cPs; **Thixotropic Index 26**.
- Calculated: 40,000 ÷ 1,500 = **26.67**.
- Proposal: at the next revision print **26.7** — or state the index as truncated, or give the shear rates used — whichever matches the measurement record.
- *Decision needed:* which of the three values is the measured one (the index, or either viscosity).
- Website meanwhile: shows "26 (as printed)" and does not publish 26.7.

## 3. GP Gelcoat Resin TDS (SPR-TDS-GGR Rev. 01) contradicts itself on appearance

- The description and key features say "water-white finish"; the property table says Colour / Appearance "White", and the title says "White, High-Gloss".
- Proposal: replace "water-white" with "white" in the description and features.
- *Decision needed:* confirm white.
- Website meanwhile: says white, following the title and the table.

## 4. Sheet Grade Yellow vs UV Stabilized Sheet Grade Yellow

- SPR-TDS-SGY (July 2026) lists "UV-resistant, holds colour over time" for the standard grade.
- SPR-TDS-USY (September 2026) says the standard grade is supplied "without a UV-stabiliser package".
- Proposal: at the next SGY revision, remove "UV-resistant", or state what it is based on.

## 5. Published SDS for Bisphenol Resin (SPR-SDS-BPR) and Fiberglass Epoxy Resin (SPR-SDS-FER) contain internal drafting notes

Both public SDS cover pages carry authoring notes, such as "resolve with Jaspal" and "that TDS was not provided in this session". They also call the two products a near-duplicate. The SDS were not edited (standing instruction).

- Proposal: reissue both SDS without the drafting notes.
- *Decision needed from the issuer:* are Bisphenol Resin and Fiberglass Epoxy Resin separate formulations, or two grades of one epoxy base?
- Website meanwhile: each product page carries a comparison FAQ that states only what the two TDS print.
