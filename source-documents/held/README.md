# Held documents

Files here were published product documents that were taken off the website
because their content did not belong to the product they were linked from.
They are kept byte-for-byte for the record. Nothing in this folder is built or
served.

## gp-white-resin-tds.pdf

- Printed title: GP WHITE RESIN. Printed reference: **SPR-TDS-GCR**, Rev. 01,
  July 2026 — GP Clear Resin's document reference.
- Appearance "Clear"; viscosity 350-450 cPs; gel time < 8 minutes; styrene
  33-40%; purity 99% — identical to the GP Clear Resin TDS.
- The GP White product page published different figures (<350 cPs, 6 min,
  medium thixotropic, 30-35%) with no GP White-specific source, so the page and
  its linked "TDS" contradicted each other on every number. Both were withdrawn
  on 2026-09-13.
- The GP White SDS (public/sds/gp-white-resin-sds.pdf) was not changed. It
  cites a TDS reference SPR-TDS-GWR, which does not appear on any issued TDS.

**Needed to restore:** a GP White-specific TDS with its own reference and the
grade's own measured values.
