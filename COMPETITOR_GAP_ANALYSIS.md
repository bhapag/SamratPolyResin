## 2. Benchmark teardown — Revex (`revex.co.in`)

The page that ranks for *"unsaturated polyester resin suppliers in India / GP resin manufacturers"* is Revex's **homepage**.

| Element | Revex | Samrat (`.in`) | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title | *"FRP GP Resin & Unsaturated Polyester Manufacturer India"* — **keyword-first, no brand** | *"Samrat Poly Resins \| Polyester, Epoxy & FRP Resin Manufacturer"* — **brand-first** | ❌ Samrat spends its most valuable pixels on a brand nobody searches yet |
| H1 | *"India's Trusted Manufacturer of FRP Resin & Unsaturated Polyester Since 1981"* | *"Polyester, Epoxy & Composite Resin Manufacturer"* | ❌ No "India", no since-year, no trust qualifier |
| Capacity | **30,000 MT/yr**, 3rd plant (60,000 MT) in development | Not stated | ❌ Major entity gap |
| Grades | **"60 grades"** | 31 products | ⚠️ Smaller, but Samrat's are individually documented |
| Certifications | **ARAI, IRS, IS-6746, EN** (product/industry approvals) | ISO 9001:2015 + GST only | ❌ Samrat has QMS cert, not *product* approvals |
| Track record | 40+ years, 1,000+ companies, <0.1% rejection | Est. 2017 | ❌ Structural — cannot be closed, must be sidestepped |
| **TDS downloads** | **None visible** | **Full TDS + SDS library** | ✅ **Samrat advantage** |
| Technical content | Thin | **213 articles + 37 application pages** | ✅ **Samrat's biggest advantage** |
| Application pages | Industry mentions only | **37 dedicated pages** | ✅ **Samrat advantage** |
| Prices | None | None (but on Samrat's own `.com`: yes) | — |

### Read-through
Revex wins on **entity mass** (age, capacity, approvals, scale). Samrat cannot manufacture that — 2017 is 2017, and inventing capacity or certifications is off the table.

Samrat wins on **documentation depth and technical specificity** — and that advantage is *already* producing results (see §4). The strategy must lean into it rather than imitate Revex.

## 6. Honest assessment of what is winnable

| Ambition | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Outrank IndiaMART for head commercial terms | ❌ Not realistic. Directories own these |
| Beat Revex on "manufacturer" entity signals | ❌ Not within 12 months. 1981 vs 2017, 30,000 MT vs undisclosed |
| Own technical/comparison/selection intent for polyester & FRP resins | ✅ **Already happening. Highly winnable** |
| Own application-specific intent (`resin for X`) | ✅ Winnable — assets exist, need linking + depth |
| Own grade-level intent (`GP clear resin`, `sheet grade yellow resin`) | ✅ Winnable — strongest product pages (median 1,774 words) |
| Rank `.in` #1 for its own brand | ✅ Winnable, fastest ROI, blocked only by the `.com` decision |
| Win "resin price" queries | ⚠️ Conditional on Q1 price-disclosure decision |
| Win national "near me" | ❌ Proximity-driven. Only real facilities/GBP move this |

**Strategic conclusion:** Samrat should **not** try to become Revex. It should become **the most technically documented resin supplier in India** — TDS/SDS transparency, grade-level specificity, application depth, comparison authority — and then convert that authority into commercial pages that currently do not exist or are starved of internal links.
